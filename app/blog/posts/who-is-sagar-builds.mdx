# Who is sagar_builds?

If you've come across **@sagar_builds** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sagar_builds/), [X (Twitter)](https://x.com/sagar_builds), or [GitHub](https://github.com/SAGAR-TAMANG), you've probably seen the builds — AI tools running on Android phones, MCP servers scaffolded in 30 seconds, full-stack apps shipped overnight.

**sagar_builds** is the internet identity of **Sagar Tamang** — an AI engineer, product builder, and content creator based in Bangalore, India.

## The Origin of sagar_builds

The name is literal. Sagar builds things.

Not plans. Not pitch decks. Not wireframes that sit in Figma for six months. **Actual working products** — shipped, deployed, and open for the world to use.

The underscore in **sagar_builds** started as a Twitter handle because `sagarbuilds` was already taken. But it stuck, because it captures the philosophy perfectly: **Sagar** (the person) + **builds** (the verb, the output, the identity).

Every piece of content on [@sagar_builds](https://x.com/sagar_builds) follows one rule: **show the build, not just the idea**.

## The Philosophy

> **"I build fast."**

That's the tagline on [sagartamang.com](https://sagartamang.com), and it's not marketing. It's a constraint.

sagar_builds ships by imposing ruthless constraints:
- **No overengineering** — the simplest solution that works, deployed today
- **Build in public** — every project gets a reel, a blog post, or a GitHub repo
- **Learn by building** — tutorials on this blog aren't theoretical; they come from real builds
- **Android-first experimentation** — if it can run on a phone, it can run anywhere
