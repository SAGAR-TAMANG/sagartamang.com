As an AI Engineer, here's what my Desk looks like.

### Technologies Used

- **📱 Samsung S24 FE** — Shot a 2-minute walkthrough video clip of my desk.
- **⚙️ FFmpeg** — Converted the video into individual frames at 3 fps.
- **🎮 Epic Games' RealityScan** — Generated a `transformers.json` file containing accurate camera coordinates for each frame.
- **🖌️ Brush** — Used the camera coordinates and frames to train the 3D Gaussian Splatting model.
- **🌐 SuperSplat** — Created annotations on the trained splat and deployed it to the world.
