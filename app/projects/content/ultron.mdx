> **"A voice with hands."**

**ULTRON** is my Iron Man-inspired AI: a holographic orb you can spin and zoom with bare-hand gestures through your webcam, a real-time voice agent you talk to like a person — and, unlike most voice assistants, it acts in the physical world. Ask it to unlock a phone, open YouTube, or run a search, and the screens in front of you light up and do it.

The demo reel blew up on release — **20,000+ likes within the first 5 hours** of publishing.

## The Idea

Voice assistants are everywhere, but they all live behind glass: they talk, they fetch information, and that's where it ends. I wanted the Tony Stark experience — an AI with a *presence* (a holographic orb that reacts to its own voice), a *personality*, and *hands*: the ability to reach out and operate real devices while you watch.

## How It Works

ULTRON is three layers working together:

- **🔮 The Orb (open source):** A holographic wireframe orb built with Three.js — layered shells, a glowing core, orbiting debris, drifting code fragments, and a cinematic bloom/chromatic-aberration pass. You can spin and zoom it by pinching mid-air, tracked through the webcam with MediaPipe hand landmarks.
- **🎙️ The Voice (proprietary):** A real-time speech-to-speech agent with a low-latency WebRTC pipeline and a custom in-character audio treatment. The orb is wired to the live output audio, so it visibly listens, thinks, and "mouths" every word as it speaks.
- **🤖 The Hands (proprietary):** A device-control layer that gives the agent command over a rack of Android devices. Fast scripted actions land within a conversational beat; anything else is handed to an autonomous agent that reads the screen, taps, types, and verifies its own work — step by step until the goal is done.

## Tech Stack

- **Interface:** Next.js, Three.js (WebGL, post-processing), MediaPipe Tasks Vision (hand tracking)
- **Voice:** OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC, custom Web Audio processing (see friday for inspiration)
- **Device control:** MCP (Model Context Protocol), Python, and other tech (releasing soon - follow for updates)
